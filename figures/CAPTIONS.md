# Figure captions (draft, clean test set, D51)

**Figure 1.** Balanced accuracy on the clean test set (747 images) against the privacy budget ε (δ = 10⁻⁵) for central training with record-level and patient-level DP-SGD, and FedAvg on the three-source partition and on a Dirichlet α = 0.5 partition with 10 clients. Points are means over five seeds with t-based 95% intervals. The diamond marks FedAvg with unclipped Gaussian weight noise (σ = 0.003), which carries no formal guarantee. Patient-level DP needs ε = 64 to reach the accuracy of record-level ε = 8.

**Figure 2.** Online LiRA against FedAvg on the three-source partition, 16 shadow models per configuration, mean ROC over five target models, log axes. Left, image level (2,610 members against the clean test images). Right, Figshare patient level, image scores summed per patient (126 against 40 patients). Shaded regions lie below the false positive rate resolution. Without DP, members are identified well above chance at every false positive rate, and DP at ε ≤ 8 removes the signal, whereas weight noise at comparable accuracy does not.

**Figure 3.** (a) Cross-seed consistency of Grad-CAM (IoU of the top 10% pixels of each model's map with those of the mean map over five seeds, following Gupta et al. 2026 with seeds in place of clients) against ε for central training. (b) Consistency against clean balanced accuracy for all 115 explained models; consistency tracks accuracy (r = 0.80).

**Figure 4.** Clean balanced accuracy of FedAvg on Dirichlet α = 0.5 partitions with K = 3, 5, 10, 15 clients holding the same 2,610 images, plotted against the standard deviation of the noise in the aggregated update, σC√K/(qN). Accuracy falls with this quantity at both privacy levels; at ε = 8 the decline is masked when the learning rate is fixed at the value tuned for K = 10 (dashed).

Numbers behind every point: figures/figure_data/*.csv. Generated by scripts/make_figures.py.
